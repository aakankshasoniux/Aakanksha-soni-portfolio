About

I'm a web designer working within the City of Santa Clara's Department of Parks & Recreation, where I design, write, and maintain the pages residents rely on for programs, facilities, and services — from swim schedules and youth center registration to senior activities, community gardens, and volunteer opportunities.

My work sits at the intersection of civic communication and everyday usability: taking dense program information and shift schedules and turning them into pages that are easy to scan, easy to update, and easy for every resident to use, regardless of their comfort with technology.

  • Content design & information architecture
  • Municipal / civic web design
  • Page layout & content maintenance (CMS)
  • Accessibility-minded design
  • Cross-department content coordination
